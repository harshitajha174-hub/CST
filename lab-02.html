College Terminology

Syllabus
An outline of the subjects in a course of study or textbook.
Credit Hour
A unit of measuring educational credit, usually based on the number of hours of classroom instruction per week.
GPA
Grade Point Average, which is a numeric indicator of a student's academic performance.
Prerequisite
A specific course or requirement that a student must complete before enrolling in a more advanced class.
Office Hours
Designated times when professors are available in their offices to meet with students outside of class.
