Famous Places in India

1. The Taj Mahal, Agra

An iconic white marble monument built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his favorite wife, representing enduring love.

2. Jaipur City Palace, Rajasthan

A grand palace complex located in the heart of the Pink City that blends Rajasthani structural style with Mughal architecture.

3. Backwaters of Kerala

A beautiful network of lakes, canals, and rivers lined with palm trees, famous for tranquil traditional houseboat stays.

4. Varanasi Ghats, Uttar Pradesh

A spiritual center located along the sacred River Ganges, known for its historic steps, rituals, and evening oil lamp prayers.

5. Hampi Ruins, Karnataka

A massive UNESCO World Heritage site showcasing the stunning stone temple architecture of the ancient Vijayanagara Empire.
